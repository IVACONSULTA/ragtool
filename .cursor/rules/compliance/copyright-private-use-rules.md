# Private Use Rules for RagTool

## EU Copyright Compliance - Private Use Only

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** December 2024  
**Scope:** Private use of RagTool for document indexing and consultation  
**Legal Basis:** EU Directives 2001/29/EC, 1996/9/EC, 2019/790/EU

---

## 🟢 SAFE RULES - PRIVATE USE COMPLIANCE

### Document and Link Indexing - SAFE Practices

#### ✅ **SAFE: Lawful Source Content**

- **Rule**: Only index content from lawful sources
- **Implementation**:
  - Public domain materials
  - Openly licensed content (Creative Commons, MIT, Apache, etc.)
  - Content with explicit private use authorization
  - Content you own or have licensed for private use
- **Verification**: Document source and licensing status

#### ✅ **SAFE: Insubstantial Parts Extraction**

- **Rule**: Limit extractions to insubstantial parts of databases
- **Implementation**:
  - Extract only small, non-significant portions
  - Avoid systematic extraction of substantial content
  - Monitor extraction volume and frequency
- **Threshold**: Less than 10% of any single work or database

#### ✅ **SAFE: Private Research and Educational Use**

- **Rule**: Use for personal research, education, or non-commercial purposes
- **Implementation**:
  - Personal learning and research only
  - Non-commercial educational activities
  - Individual knowledge enhancement
- **Verification**: Maintain usage logs showing private, non-commercial use

#### ✅ **SAFE: Technical Protection Respect**

- **Rule**: Respect all technical protection measures
- **Implementation**:
  - Do not circumvent DRM or access controls
  - Respect robots.txt and similar restrictions
  - Honor website terms of service
- **Verification**: Regular compliance checks

#### ✅ **SAFE: Rights Management Information Preservation**

- **Rule**: Preserve all copyright and rights management information
- **Implementation**:
  - Keep original attribution and copyright notices
  - Maintain source URLs and publication information
  - Preserve author and publisher information
- **Verification**: Include metadata in all indexed content

### RAG Tool Usage - SAFE Practices

#### ✅ **SAFE: Private Access Only**

- **Rule**: Restrict access to authorized private users only
- **Implementation**:
  - User authentication required
  - Access logging and monitoring
  - No public or shared access
- **Verification**: Regular access audit logs

#### ✅ **SAFE: Non-Commercial Use**

- **Rule**: Use only for non-commercial purposes
- **Implementation**:
  - Personal research and learning
  - Individual decision-making support
  - Non-profit educational activities
- **Verification**: Usage purpose documentation

#### ✅ **SAFE: Fair Use Principles**

- **Rule**: Apply fair use principles to all content usage
- **Implementation**:
  - Transformative use (analysis, summarization, research)
  - Limited quantity of content used
  - No market substitution effect
- **Verification**: Regular fair use assessment

## 🔴 PROHIBITED RULES - PRIVATE USE VIOLATIONS

### Document and Link Indexing - PROHIBITED Actions

#### ❌ **PROHIBITED: Unauthorized Content Indexing**

- **Rule**: Never index content without proper authorization
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Indexing copyrighted content without permission
  - Bypassing paywalls or subscription requirements
  - Ignoring explicit opt-out requests from rights holders
  - Indexing content behind technical protection measures
- **Risk**: Copyright infringement claims and legal action

#### ❌ **PROHIBITED: Substantial Database Extraction**

- **Rule**: Never extract substantial parts of protected databases
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Systematic extraction of database contents
  - Repeated extraction of insubstantial parts
  - Commercial use of extracted database content
  - Circumventing database access controls
- **Risk**: Database rights infringement and substantial damages

#### ❌ **PROHIBITED: Commercial Use of Indexed Content**

- **Rule**: Never use indexed content for commercial purposes
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Selling or licensing indexed content
  - Using for commercial research or development
  - Providing commercial services based on indexed content
  - Monetizing the RAG tool or its outputs
- **Risk**: Commercial copyright infringement with enhanced damages

#### ❌ **PROHIBITED: Technical Protection Circumvention**

- **Rule**: Never circumvent technical protection measures
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Bypassing DRM or encryption
  - Using tools to circumvent access controls
  - Modifying or removing technical protection
  - Creating tools to facilitate circumvention
- **Risk**: Anti-circumvention law violations

### RAG Tool Usage - PROHIBITED Actions

#### ❌ **PROHIBITED: Public Distribution**

- **Rule**: Never make RAG tool or its outputs publicly available
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Sharing RAG outputs publicly
  - Making the tool accessible to unauthorized users
  - Publishing or distributing generated content
  - Creating public APIs or interfaces
- **Risk**: Communication to public copyright infringement

#### ❌ **PROHIBITED: Rights Management Information Removal**

- **Rule**: Never remove or alter rights management information
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Removing copyright notices
  - Altering author attribution
  - Modifying publication information
  - Stripping metadata or source information
- **Risk**: Rights management information violations

#### ❌ **PROHIBITED: Systematic Extraction**

- **Rule**: Never engage in systematic extraction of protected content
- **Prohibited Actions**:
  - Automated bulk content extraction
  - Systematic downloading of entire websites
  - Repeated extraction from the same sources
  - Creating comprehensive copies of protected works
- **Risk**: Systematic copyright infringement

## 🛡️ COMPLIANCE IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES

### Pre-Indexing Checklist

Before indexing any content, verify:

- [ ] Content is from a lawful source
- [ ] No technical protection measures are circumvented
- [ ] Extraction is limited to insubstantial parts
- [ ] Rights management information is preserved
- [ ] Source attribution is maintained
- [ ] Private use authorization is confirmed

### Usage Monitoring Requirements

Implement continuous monitoring for:

- [ ] Access control compliance
- [ ] Non-commercial use verification
- [ ] Extraction volume limits
- [ ] Rights management information preservation
- [ ] Technical protection respect
- [ ] User authentication and authorization

### Regular Compliance Reviews

Conduct quarterly reviews of:

- [ ] Indexed content sources and licensing
- [ ] Usage patterns and purposes
- [ ] Access controls and user management
- [ ] Rights management information integrity
- [ ] Technical protection compliance
- [ ] Fair use assessment

## ⚠️ RISK MITIGATION STRATEGIES

### High-Risk Mitigation

1. **Source Verification**: Always verify content licensing before indexing
2. **Access Controls**: Implement robust user authentication and access logging
3. **Volume Monitoring**: Track extraction volumes to prevent systematic extraction
4. **Rights Respect**: Implement opt-out mechanisms and respect rights holder requests

### Medium-Risk Mitigation

1. **Usage Documentation**: Maintain detailed logs of all usage
2. **Regular Audits**: Conduct regular compliance assessments
3. **User Education**: Provide clear guidelines to all users
4. **Technical Safeguards**: Implement technical measures to prevent violations

### Low-Risk Mitigation

1. **Best Practices**: Follow industry best practices for private use
2. **Legal Updates**: Stay informed about legal developments
3. **Professional Advice**: Consult legal professionals for complex cases
4. **Community Standards**: Follow ethical guidelines and community standards
