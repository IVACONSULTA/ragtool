Preface

The European VAT Handbook aims to provide detailed and essential information about VAT in Europe for
practitioners who need to know about VAT issues in their every day work. In addition to practitioners involved
with VAT issues, the book could can be used by students, firms or and academic institutions teaching the subject.

In this book a detailed VAT structure, in an easy-to-understand style, is given per European country to enable
comparison. The book also includes Annexes which provide a good overview of the VAT structure, rates,
thresholds, reverse charges and simplification measures applicable in the various European countries.

The European VAT Handbook consists of country chapters of 27 EU countries, namely Austria, Belgium,
Bulgaria, Croatia, Republic of Cyprus, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany,
Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal,
Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden are included in the book. Included are also the chapters of
three non-EU countries: Switzerland, Norway and the United Kingdom.

The chapters of the countries follow the standard outline and cover a.o. the following topics:
+ How and where to register
+ Registration thresholds

+ VAT grouping

+ Fiscal representation

+ When is the VAT due (new)
+ Cash accounting (new)

* Consignment and call-off stock
+ Simplified triangulation

+ Transfer of business

+ Installation supplies

+ Distance sales

+ Acquisition thresholds

+ Use & Enjoyment rules

* Bad debt relief

* Cash discounts

+ Importation and postponed accounting
* VAT warehousing

+ Customs warehousing

+ Reverse charges

+ VAT rates

+ Exemptions

* Which VAT is recoverable
+ Adjustment periods

+ Invoice requirements

+ Simplified invoices

+ Self-billing

* VAT Compliance

* EC Sales Listing

+ Intrastat

+ Penalties

This edition contains a few new subjects such as the VAT treatment of returnable packaging, liabilities and
supplies that are made free of charge (i.e. the threshold for ‘gifts of small value’).

The information per country was provided by experienced VAT advisors from other than the Big4 companies.
The names and of the authors and their contact details are provided at the end of each of the chapters
covering the relevant countries. Most of them are also partners in VAT Forum. For information about VAT
Forum, please refer to the following link: https://vatforum.com/. Vat Forum is an intemational network of
independent, seasoned VAT advisors who provide a highly appreciated alternative to the big accounting firms.
Special thanks for my colleague, Dr. Jeroen Bijl who helped me edit this edition.
